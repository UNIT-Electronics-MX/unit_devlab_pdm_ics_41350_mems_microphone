## **Description**

The UNIT DevLab PDM ICS-41350 MEMS Microphone is a compact digital-audio module
built around the bottom-port TDK InvenSense ICS-41350. The microphone contains
a MEMS element, impedance-converter amplifier, and fourth-order sigma-delta
modulator that produces a 1-bit pulse-density-modulated output.

![](hardware/resources/unit_top_v_1_1_0_pdm_ics_41350_mems_microphone.png){width=5.4in}

### **Applications**

- Voice capture and recognition prototypes
- Microphone-array development
- Camera, security, and surveillance audio
- Low-power ambient sound analysis and keyword spotting
- Ultrasonic sensing experiments in high-performance mode

### **Hardware Features**

- ICS-41350 bottom-port digital MEMS microphone
- PDM clock and data interface
- Clock-selected low-power, standard, high-performance, and sleep modes
- Sensor channel selection for shared-data stereo configurations
- AP2112K-3.3TRG1 fixed 3.3 V regulator identified by the BOM
- Six 2.54 mm edge/header connections
- Four-position, 1.0 mm-pitch right-angle connector
- Orange indicator LED and two solder options shown on the V1.1 artwork
