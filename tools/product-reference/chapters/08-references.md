The local microphone source is TDK InvenSense document DS-000047, revision
1.1, dated 2016-06-23. The sensor characteristics in this reference follow
that datasheet. The module connections follow the UE0148 schematic and
pinout, and the visible labels follow the V1.1 board artwork.
