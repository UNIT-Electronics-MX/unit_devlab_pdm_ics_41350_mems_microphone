## **6 Mechanical Information**

![](hardware/resources/unit_top_v_1_1_0_pdm_ics_41350_mems_microphone.png){width=5.4in}

The rendered V1.1 artwork shows six castellated edge connections, a
four-position right-angle connector, and a central circular opening. A
controlled dimensional drawing and mounting coordinates are not available, so
dimensions must not be scaled from the render.

The ICS-41350 itself uses a 3.5 mm × 2.65 mm × 0.98 mm bottom-port package.
Those sensor-package dimensions are not the dimensions of the module. Allow
clearance around the acoustic path and keep it free of adhesive, debris, and
mounting obstruction.
